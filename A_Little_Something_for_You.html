The moment I knew it was you. ♡

I still remember how heartbroken I was after my CAT exam. I was so disappointed that I almost didn't want to give my NMAT exam. I had pretty much given up on myself, but you didn't let me. You cheered me up, stayed on call with me and were there with me all the way until I reached the exam centre. I don't think you even realize how much that meant to me.

I ended up scoring 224, and that score helped me get into the MBA college I am in today. Sometimes I think about how different things could have been if you hadn't encouraged me that day. You gave me a reason to try when I didn't have one myself. And somewhere in that moment, I realized it was you. It was always going to be you. ❤️

Some moments stay with you because of how someone made you feel. I'll always remember how you showed up for me that day.

And yes, I'm still very grateful you didn't let me give up. ❤️
